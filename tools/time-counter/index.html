Time Counter

Plan and record a school year of work time, and see how it tracks against the instructional and assignable time limits in the ATA/CBE collective agreement.

This tool is being built in stages. The counting engine (calendar, day types, bell times and the counting rules) is in place; the screens come next. Run the engine self-test.

Time Counter gives an estimate against clause 8.1 of the ATA/CBE collective agreement. It is a reference, not an official ruling. See the collective agreement and the ATA assignable and instructional time calculators. Questions: ATA Local 38, 403-262-6616, info@ata38.ab.ca.
